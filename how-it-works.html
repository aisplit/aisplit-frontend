How it works

Three steps.Zero friction.

From expense to settled — in seconds. No spreadsheets, no awkward conversations, no math.

01

Set up once. Use forever.

Create a group

Create a group for your trip, household, or office. Add members by name or phone number. AISplit remembers everyone so you never have to re-enter names.

  • Name your group (e.g. "Goa Trip 2026")
  • Add members by name or phone
  • Set a default currency for the group
  • Invite others to join via link
02

Natural language. Instant results.

Type or speak an expense

Just describe the expense in plain English or Hindi. AISplit's AI parses who paid, how much, and how to split — no forms, no dropdowns.

  • "Rahul paid 500 for dinner, split equally"
  • "I paid 1200 for hotel, Priya owes 600"
  • "Taxi 300 rupees, split 3 ways"
  • Voice input supported — just tap the mic
03

Parsed in milliseconds.

AI splits instantly

The AI extracts names, amounts, and split logic from your natural language input and calculates each person's share automatically.

  • Identifies payer and participants
  • Calculates equal or custom splits
  • Handles complex splits (percentages, fixed amounts)
  • Shows a clear breakdown before confirming
04

Always know who owes what.

Track balances

AISplit keeps a running tally of all balances across the group. See exactly who owes whom at any point — no mental math required.

  • Real-time balance updates
  • Per-person breakdown
  • Group total and individual totals
  • History of all expenses
05

Pay without leaving the app.

Settle via UPI

When it's time to settle up, pay directly via UPI from within AISplit. No switching apps, no copying IDs. One tap and you're done.

  • Direct UPI payment in-app
  • Supports all major UPI apps
  • Instant settlement confirmation
  • Balances update automatically after payment
