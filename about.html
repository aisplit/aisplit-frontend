Our story

Built out offrustration.

We were tired of spreadsheets, awkward "who owes what" conversations, and expense apps that cost more than the dinner we were splitting.

AISplit started as a weekend project. A group of friends came back from a trip with a mess of expenses, a broken spreadsheet, and three different "final" totals. There had to be a better way.

We built AISplit to be the app we always wanted — one where you just say what happened and it figures out the rest. No forms. No dropdowns. No math. Just natural language and instant results.

We made it free because expense splitting shouldn't cost money. We added Hindi support because great tools should work in your language. We made it offline-first because not every trip has good signal.

AISplit is built for India — for trips to Goa, for roommates in Bangalore, for office lunches in Mumbai. It's fast, it's smart, and it's completely free.
